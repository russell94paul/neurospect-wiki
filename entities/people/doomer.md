# dOoMeR (Aura Mentor)

Instructor of the **Aura** mentorship — a second, independent ICT / Smart Money Concepts
teacher whose corpus is being ingested into this wiki to cross-check, deepen, and enrich the
existing [[entities/people/mrwitness-axl]] notes. YouTube channel **dOoMeR** (`@doomer312`).
The playlist ingested here is titled **"Aura"** (also the name of a proprietary concept in the
framework — the *Aura Asset*).

> This page is preliminary — populated from the Phase 0/1 transcription pass. It will be
> deepened during Phase 2 standalone synthesis (`concepts/aura/`). See
> [[processes/distributed-workflow/active/aura-ingest]].

## Teaching Approach

- ICT / Smart Money Concepts foundation, but delivered through a **distinct proprietary
  framework and vocabulary** (see below) rather than restating canonical ICT terms.
- **Psychology-first curriculum.** The mentorship opens with five sessions on mindset,
  emotional control, discipline systems, and journaling *before* any technical concept —
  a notable contrast with the MrWitness-AXL corpus, which is almost entirely technical.
  Opens Day 1 with a James Clear / *Atomic Habits* framing: "you do not rise to the level
  of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems." (aura-01)
- Structured paid mentorship (surveyed 142 students before launch) later reposted to
  YouTube; content runs concept lessons → applied trade/market reviews. (aura-01)

## Proprietary Vocabulary (to be defined in Phase 2)

Terms that appear repeatedly in the corpus and are candidates for their own `concepts/aura/`
pages. Definitions are **not yet extracted** — do not assume they match ICT canon:

- **Sequential SMT** — a sequenced take on SMT divergence (aura-07, 11, 12, 14).
- **Sequential Skip** — related variation/exception to Sequential SMT (aura-14).
- **Aura Asset** — the framework's signature concept; also the playlist name (aura-15, 16, 17).
- **Time Sum** — time-based concept paired with the Aura Asset (aura-16).
- **Triads / asset selection** — which correlated assets to watch and why (aura-10).
- **Ranges** and **Gaps** — treated as first-class structural concepts (aura-08, 09, 11).
