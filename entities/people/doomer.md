# dOoMeR (Aura Mentor)

Instructor of the **Aura** mentorship — a second, independent ICT / Smart Money Concepts teacher
whose corpus was ingested into this wiki to cross-check, deepen, and enrich the existing
[[entities/people/mrwitness-axl]] notes. YouTube channel **dOoMeR** (`@doomer312`). The playlist
ingested here is titled **"Aura"** — also the name of his signature concept, the *Aura Asset*.

The Aura material was originally a **paid mentorship** (dOoMeR states he surveyed 142 prospective
students before building it) later reposted to YouTube as 30 videos (~12 hrs). The curriculum runs
psychology → technical framework → applied trade/market reviews. Full synthesis lives in
[[concepts/aura/README]]; this page is the person/entity summary.

## Teaching Approach

- **Psychology-first.** The mentorship spends its first five sessions on mindset, emotional control,
  discipline systems, and journaling *before* any chart concept — a sharp contrast with the
  MrWitness-AXL corpus, which is almost entirely technical. Opens Day 1 with a James Clear /
  *Atomic Habits* framing: "you do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your
  systems" (aura-01). See [[concepts/aura/psychology-foundations]].
- **Minimalist vocabulary.** dOoMeR deliberately strips ICT down to three structural primitives —
  **swing points → ranges → gaps** — and explicitly *rejects* order-block / PDA terminology: "only
  highs and lows, ranges and gaps are important in the market... I don't use order block" (aura-08).
  His own compact summary: "Ranges, gaps, what lies within, liquidity, highs and lows, swing points"
  (aura-09).
- **Math-first, then chart-validated.** Every correlated-asset "triad" is selected with a **Pearson
  correlation coefficient on 2–3 years of daily returns** before he ever looks at a chart — "the math
  came first. The data backs it up" — but he is emphatic the number is "a place to start," not the
  end, and the live chart overrules it when they disagree (aura-10). See
  [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]].
- **Empirical / data-driven.** His distinctive "what lies within" gap concept came from his own
  data-collection — "precisely from the data that I collected... where did price go, looking left at
  precise levels" (aura-09). Journaling is framed the same way: the journal is a data-collection
  system, "the difference between a trader and a gambler" (aura-05).
- **Probabilistic framing.** He repeatedly stresses his edges happen "a large percent of the time,"
  not every time — "within trading you don't need something that happens every time... you need
  something that happens a higher percentage of the time" (aura-07), paired with strict risk control.

## The Aura Framework (proprietary vocabulary, now synthesized)

Each term below has a full page in `concepts/aura/`:

|---|---|---|

| Triads / asset selection | Asset sets chosen by Pearson correlation; 5 triads | [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]] |
| **Sequential SMT** | SMT confirmed across ≥2 nested time cycles; his signature technical concept | [[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]] |
| **Sequential Skip** | Fallback confirmation when the adjacent cycle link is missing | [[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]] |
| **Aura Asset** | Swiss Franc futures (6S) as a universal 4th triad leg / dollar-proxy | [[concepts/aura/aura-asset]] |
| Time Sum | Digital-root "369" timestamp check — *he de-emphasizes it himself* | [[concepts/aura/time-sum]] |
| HTF→LTF workflow | Cascading if-then cycle chain down to a 5m FVG/iFVG trigger | [[concepts/aura/htf-ltf-application]] |

His **signature contribution** is the Aura Asset: the Swiss Franc futures contract (6S) added to every
triad as a synchronized dollar-proxy divergence leg, chosen over DXY because DXY is not a
time-synchronized futures contract (aura-15). Its *origin story* (a Switzerland/"elite bloodlines"
thesis) he presents as an explicitly unverified personal theory, separate from the quantitative work.

## Intellectual Influences (named in the corpus)

- **Tom Dante** — the dominant influence on the psychology/risk/journaling material ("Blueprint for
  Trading Success"; the canceled-order edge anecdote; the three review questions). See
  [[concepts/aura/risk-management]], [[concepts/aura/journaling-system]].
- **Mark Douglas** (*Trading in the Zone*) and **James Clear** (*Atomic Habits*) — mindset and
  habit-formation framing.
- **Andrew Huberman** — sleep/dopamine claims (auto-captions render "Human"; corrected).
- **Napoleon Hill** — the "accurate thinking" idea applied to journaling (aura-05).
- **Fritz Springmeier** (*Bloodlines of the Illuminati*) — cited only for the Aura Asset origin essay,
  and explicitly flagged by dOoMeR as speculative (aura-15).
- An **earlier ICT/SMT mentor** he credits as the source of the original (non-Sequential) SMT idea,
  transcribed only as "DA's teaching" — the actual name is not recoverable from the captions (aura-07).

## Honesty Markers

dOoMeR is unusually candid about the limits of his own material, which raises confidence in the rest:

- Frames the **Aura Asset origin story** as "conspiracy theories I have put together on my own... I am
  not presenting any of what follows as fact" — separate from the chart-based validation (aura-15).
- **De-emphasizes "Time Sum"/369** himself as not load-bearing (aura-16), captured honestly rather
  than inflated.
- Discloses the **triad correlation document** was adapted from a compliance/research write-up for the
  prop firm he trades at, and that some of its "institutional behavior" language is borrowed packaging
  he doesn't fully endorse (aura-10).
