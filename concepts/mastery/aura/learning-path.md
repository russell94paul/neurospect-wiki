# Aura Learning Path

The sequenced route from "I've watched the videos" to "I'm cleared to live-trade the Aura model" — the
concrete curriculum for [[concepts/mastery/README]]'s ladder and readiness gate. dOoMeR's own corpus is
**psychology-first then technical**, and this path keeps that order deliberately: the discipline layer is
the foundation the technical edge is executed on, not an afterthought.

Each stage lists the **concepts** to learn (link = the *what/why*), the **drills** to run (link =
[[concepts/mastery/aura/exercises]], the *how*), and the **gate** that must clear before moving on. Track
progress in [[concepts/mastery/aura/tracker]].

> **Don't skip ahead.** Every technical concept is built on the one before it (swing points → ranges →
> gaps → SMT). dOoMeR: "the same thing you were doing on day one of marking out swing points... helps you
> find the range." Marching in order is the point.

## Stage 0 — Foundation: psychology & discipline (Aura videos 1–5)

The mentorship spends its first five sessions here before any chart concept. So do you.

- **Concepts:** [[concepts/aura/psychology-foundations]] · [[concepts/aura/mind-and-emotional-control]] (four killers, circuit breakers) · [[concepts/aura/discipline-systems]] (systems > willpower; pre/post-market routine) · [[concepts/aura/journaling-system]]
- **Drills:** D0-a routine + environment build · D0-b identity statements + observer practice · D0-c circuit-breaker rules written & visible · D0-d journal set up (see [[concepts/mastery/aura/exercises]] §Stage 0)
- **Rules established:** [R47–R54].
- **Gate:** pre-market 3-question routine + circuit breakers written and **actually followed for one week**;
  journal live. This is behavioural, not chart-skill — but it is a hard prerequisite for touching size later.

## Stage 1 — The three structural primitives (videos 6, 8, 9)

The heart of the eye-training. This is where hand-marking matters most.

- **1a Swing points** — [[concepts/aura/swing-points]] · Drill **D1-a**: mark ≥50 swings on D/4H/1H, then
  2nd pass recolour SMT-qualified vs not. **[R1–R3]**
- **1b Ranges** — [[concepts/aura/ranges]] · Drill **D1-b**: mark ≥50 ranges (expansive-move method), then
  refine anchors to SMT-qualified swings; forward-bar "live read" simulation. **[R4–R10]**
- **1c Gaps & what lies within** — [[concepts/aura/gaps]] · Drill **D1-c**: within your ranges (NQ+YM), mark
  liquidity left/right of gaps + the liquidity *inside* the gap. **[R11–R14]**
- **Gate:** each primitive at **Can-mark** (Stage 2 of the ladder), reps ≥50 each, confidence ≥3. You can
  mark all three **by hand** on an unlabelled chart before any indicator is switched on.

## Stage 2 — The confirmation engine (videos 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15)

Where the model becomes a *decision system*, not just structure.

- **2a Triads & asset selection** — [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]] · Drill **D2-a**: recompute
  Pearson correlation on daily returns for one triad; validate the sweet-spot against the live chart. **[R15–R16]**
- **2b Aura Asset (6S)** — [[concepts/aura/aura-asset]] · Drill **D2-b**: overlay 6S and find divergences the
  standard triad missed. **[R17]**
- **2c Sequential SMT** — [[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]] · Drill **D2-c**: the NY-open drill + bar-by-bar
  range flip (~50 reps); mark by hand, then check vs the indicator. **[R18–R23]**
- **2d Sequential Skip** — same page §Skip · Drill **D2-d**: hunt skip setups (down-cycle + cross-asset). **[R24–R26]**
- **Gate:** Sequential SMT + Skip at **Can-mark**, confidence ≥3; you can state, for a given swing, whether
  it's SMT-qualified and across which cycles — *before* looking at the indicator.

## Stage 3 — Putting it together: the cascade & risk (videos 13, 16, 17)

- **3a HTF→LTF cascade** — [[concepts/aura/htf-ltf-application]] · Drill **D3-a**: define confirm-AND-
  invalidate before every practice entry; run the full top-down chain to a 5m iFVG. **[R27–R32]**
- **3b Time Sum** — [[concepts/aura/time-sum]] · (optional, de-emphasized — awareness only). **[R37]**
- **3c Risk management** — [[concepts/aura/risk-management]] · Drill **D3-c**: R / expectancy / break-even
  math reps; evolving-R trade-management reps. **[R38–R46]**
- **Gate:** you can run a full framing→entry→stop→target plan end to end using
  [[concepts/mastery/aura/checklist]], and compute the trade's expectancy contribution in R.

## Stage 4 — Backtest in bar-replay (build the sample & the expectancy number)

The transition from "can mark" to "has an edge on evidence."

- **Drills:** **D4-a** study-then-replicate the 13 worked trade reviews on their exact replay dates
  (043026, 050126, 112625, 121125, 050726, 051226, 051326, 042625, 05212026 — see
  [[concepts/mastery/aura/exercises]] §Stage 4 and [[concepts/aura/trade-reviews]]); **D4-b** blind
  forward-replay on unseen dates, full checklist per setup, logged in R.
- **Gate:** ≥50 setups (ideally ≥100 executed backtest trades) logged with **positive expectancy in R** and
  a known win-rate-with-R:R clearing break-even. Every core concept at **Backtested**.

## Stage 5 — Live tape reading (real-time, no capital)

Reading price as it forms, not in hindsight — the skill replay can't fully teach.

- **Drills:** **D5-a** live/delayed tape-reading sessions on the triad + 6S, calling the read aloud/in-notes
  before it resolves; **D5-b** sim execution with the full checklist. *(ICT-track tape-reading stream
  transcripts also serve as narrated reference — Phase 2.)*
- **Gate:** a demo/sim track record showing the plan + circuit-breaker discipline hold under live pressure;
  core concepts at **Live-ready**.

## Stage 6 — Journal-driven refinement (continuous)

Runs alongside Stages 4–5 and never stops.

- **Drills:** **D6-a** weekly review on Dante's three questions; **D6-b** the active-manage-vs-walk-away
  comparison ("your personal holy grail"); **D6-c** missed/canceled-trade log (Dante's canceled-order edge).
- **Gate:** journaling habit established (every trade incl. missed; never-miss-twice holding); at least one
  concrete rule change made *from your own logged data*.

## The finish line — Readiness-to-Live Gate

When Stages 0–6 are green, run the full [[concepts/mastery/README]] **Readiness-to-Live Gate**. Only when
every box there is honestly checked do you move to live capital — and even then, **start small**.
