# Unified Mastery Tracker

**This is a living page — Paul edits it as he progresses.** It is the single place that answers "where am
I, and am I ready to go live?" for the [[concepts/mastery/unified/README|Unified Playbook]]. Concepts are
grouped by [[concepts/mastery/unified/learning-path|learning-path]] stage; scales are defined once in
[[concepts/mastery/README]] and reused:

- **Ladder:** `1 Learned` → `2 Can-mark` → `3 Backtested` → `4 Live-ready`
- **Confidence:** `1` no feel · `2` shaky · `3` usually right · `4` reliable · `5` automatic
- Fill the ladder cell with the current stage number; tick reps as you go; date the last practice.

> All cells start blank/`0` — nothing is claimed until it's true (the tracker is evidence, not aspiration).
> **Drills are not forked here** — the "Drill" column links to the canonical drill in the two exercise
> libraries. Reps/targets mirror those libraries; log against the same reps you log there.

## Readiness-to-Live Gate

Copied by reference from [[concepts/mastery/README]] §Readiness-to-Live Gate — that page is canonical; this
is the unified track's live copy. **Do not move to live capital until every box is honestly checked.** Even
then, **start small** — the gate certifies readiness to begin, not to size up. **No frontier (U5) concept
counts toward "core concept at Backtested+"** — the gate is cleared on the established playbook (U1–U4), not
on unbacktested confluence.

- [ ] Every core concept (U1–U4) at **Backtested+**; the load-bearing few at **Live-ready**.
- [ ] Backtest sample met (≥50 setups / ≥100 trades) with **positive expectancy in R** (evidence in the per-track trackers).
- [ ] Win rate known **with its R:R** and clearing break-even for that R:R.
- [ ] Risk rules precommitted **in writing**: 1–2% per trade, 2–3R daily stop, 10R circuit-breaker (or the prop-account dollar-cap equivalent, R5), no sizing up in drawdown.
- [ ] Demo/sim track record long enough to show discipline holds live (not just in hindsight).
- [ ] Journaling habit established — every trade incl. missed/canceled; weekly review; never-miss-twice holding.
- [ ] Circuit-breaker discipline **demonstrated** under real pressure (actually stopped at the daily/2-loss stop).
