# Unified Model — Divergence Rulings

This page is the analytical spine of the [[concepts/mastery/unified/README|Unified Playbook]]. It takes
each place where the two ingested mentors — **MrWitness-AXL** ([[concepts/course/README|ICT-course]]) and
**dOoMeR** ([[concepts/aura/README|Aura]]) — diverge, and issues **one ruling** per divergence:
*supersedes*, *coexist (layered)*, or *drop*. Each ruling is justified from the source material and cited.

> **No-drift:** this page does not restate what a concept *is* — the canonical `ict-*` / `aura-*` pages
> own that. It only records the reconciliation decision and links to the evidence. Where the wiki already
> carries a divergence flag, this page **resolves** that flag (or explicitly declines to) rather than
> re-describing it.

## Ruling types

- **SUPERSEDE** — one framing is strictly better for our purpose (NQ/ES/YM intraday); adopt it, retire the other.
- **COEXIST (LAYERED)** — both are correct at *different altitudes or stages*; the unified model runs them in sequence, and their agreement is itself a confluence.
- **DROP** — a mentor already de-emphasizes it; not load-bearing; excluded from the core playbook.

## R1 — SMT role: confluence tool vs. primary structural signal → **COEXIST (LAYERED)** ⭐

The single most important reconciliation. Source flag: [[concepts/business-logic/ict-smt]] (SMT-role
divergence, "not merged"); [[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]].

- **AXL:** SMT is a **confluence tool** at the Power-of-Three *manipulation leg* — it confirms a
  pre-existing bias at a single swing on a single timeframe ([[concepts/business-logic/ict-smt]] §The Triad).
- **Aura:** **Sequential SMT** — SMT filtered through time-cycle nesting — is elevated to the **primary
  structural signal** that anchors and flips entire ranges, *ahead of* any separate bias step
  ([[concepts/business-logic/ict-smt]] §Aura Extension: Sequential SMT).

**Ruling — they are not rivals; they operate at different altitudes and compose into one signal chain:**

1. **Anchor (HTF, structural):** use Aura **Sequential SMT** across nested cycles to decide *which swing
   points are load-bearing* and therefore where the dealing range and bias are anchored. This is a
   bias-*setting* step.
2. **Trigger (LTF, confluence):** at the manipulation leg where you actually enter, use AXL **same-timeframe
   triad SMT** as the confluence that the sweep is complete. This is a bias-*confirming* step.

> **New confluence — the nested SMT stack.** Highest conviction occurs when a same-TF triad SMT at the
> entry leg sits *inside* a confirmed higher-cycle Sequential SMT pointing the same direction. AXL's use
> becomes the entry-timing lens; Aura's use becomes the structural lens; agreement across both is a
> stronger signal than either alone. Neither mentor states this explicitly — it is the reconciliation's
> first genuine synthesis.

## R2 — Swing qualification: FVG-filter vs. SMT-filter → **COEXIST (LAYERED)** ⭐

Source flag: [[concepts/business-logic/ict-market-structure]] §Aura — "two different lenses ... not a
contradiction to be resolved." Both filter the *same* 3-candle pivot (a stated **convergence**).

- **AXL:** a swing becomes ITH/ITL (trustworthy) only when its leg **fills or creates a FVG** — a
  single-asset, price-structure test.
- **Aura:** a swing is significant only when **SMT-qualified** — an intermarket, correlation test.

**Ruling — run both filters; grade the swing by how many it passes.**

> **New confluence — the double-qualified swing.** A swing point that is *both* FVG-qualified (AXL) *and*
> SMT-qualified (Aura) is the highest-conviction structural anchor in the unified model — protect it, build
> ranges off it, and treat its violation as a genuine structure shift. A swing passing only one filter is
> tradeable but second-tier; one passing neither is "suspect" (AXL's word) and ignored. This turns two
> "competing" filters into a single 0-1-2 conviction score.

## R3 — Range subdivision: quadrants vs. discount/EQ/premium → **COEXIST (LAYERED)**

Source flag: [[concepts/business-logic/ict-order-flow]] (quadrants, 0/.25/.5/.75/1) vs.
[[concepts/aura/ranges]] (discount/EQ/premium only, no quadrants); mirrored on
[[concepts/business-logic/ict-liquidity]].

**Ruling — different resolutions of the same axis; use the coarse one for bias, the fine one for targeting.**

- **Discount / EQ / premium** (the three-zone view) is the **primitive** — it answers the bias question
  "am I buying cheap or selling expensive relative to EQ?" Use it first, always.
- **Quadrants** (0.25 / 0.75) are an **optional refinement** for *PDA placement and target precision*
  inside the discount or premium half. Use them when you need a sharper level than "somewhere below EQ."

They describe one number line at two resolutions; there is no contradiction to resolve, only a decision
about when finer granularity earns its keep.

## R4 — "Order block" / PDA vocabulary breadth → **COEXIST (SUPERSET)**

Source flag: [[concepts/entry-models/README]] §Aura (superset relationship, "not merged");
[[concepts/business-logic/ict-liquidity]] (both mentors point at the same origination level, no shared word).

- **AXL:** rich PDA set — order block, breaker, rejection block, OTE block, CSD, BPR.
- **Aura:** deliberately minimal — rejects "order block"; enters off **FVG / iFVG only** ("all I'm looking
  for are expansive moves").

**Ruling — minimal set is the default; the richer set is the situational superset.**

- **Default execution vocabulary is Aura's FVG/iFVG** — fewer moving parts, fewer ways to rationalize a bad
  entry, and it is what the [[concepts/entry-models/smt-confirmation-entry]] model already uses.
- **The richer AXL PDA set is available when the situation calls for it** — Model 2022 OTE
  ([[concepts/entry-models/model-2022-ote]]), breaker/rejection reversals. These are the *same origination
  levels* named more finely; adding a name is allowed, but the entry still resolves to "did an FVG/iFVG form
  at this level?" Both vocabularies are kept; neither is discarded.

## R5 — Risk philosophy: fixed-dollar cap vs. %/R Blueprint → **SUPERSEDE (%/R)** ⭐

**This divergence was not previously flagged anywhere in the wiki** — surfaced during Phase 3 research and
recorded here for the first time (per [[CLAUDE]] Rule #6, flag, don't silently merge).

- **AXL:** a **fixed-dollar** daily loss cap scaled by account size — $1,000/day if ≥$10K, $500/day if
  smaller; "hit the limit → log out" ([[concepts/business-logic/ict-live-commentary]] §Live Trading Discipline).
- **Aura / Dante Blueprint:** a **percentage / R-multiple** system — 1–2% per trade, 2–3R daily stop, 10R
  drawdown circuit-breaker, expectancy in R ([[concepts/aura/risk-management]]).

**Ruling — the %/R Blueprint supersedes as the framework; AXL's fixed-dollar cap is a special case of it.**

- Adopt the **R-multiple** system as canonical: per-trade risk 1–2% of *total* capital, daily stop 2–3R,
  10R circuit-breaker, positive-expectancy gate — because R-based rules scale across account sizes and are
  what the [[concepts/mastery/README|Readiness-to-Live Gate]] already measures.
- AXL's fixed-dollar cap is **not wrong — it is the same daily-stop principle expressed in dollars** because
  prop-firm accounts *impose* a dollar limit. When trading such an account, convert: daily-stop-in-R × per-trade-$risk
  should land at or under the firm's dollar cap. The two are compatible once you see the dollar cap as a
  prop-imposed expression of the 2–3R daily stop.
- Carry forward Aura's **firm-standard** stop-trailing resolution (treat the tight firm standard as the
  target, not dOoMeR's looser personal account — [[concepts/mastery/aura/rules]] R-flag).

  "liquidity within the gap" ([[concepts/aura/gaps]]).
