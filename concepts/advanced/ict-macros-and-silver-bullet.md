# ICT Macros, Killzone Micro-Structure & Silver Bullet

Three frontier time-concepts that are really **one model viewed at three zoom levels**. They extend — never
restate — the canonical killzone page [[concepts/course/module-3-session-and-bias/02-session-kill-zones]],
adding the *intra-killzone micro-timing* that page does not cover. They plug into
[[concepts/mastery/unified/README]] Layer 3 as the **WHEN** filter.

```
Killzone  (hour-scale window)  → contains Macro(s) (~20-min sub-windows)
   → where the Judas Swing (manipulation leg) is expected to reverse via an MSS
      → Silver Bullet = the FVG-retracement entry rule for that reversal, bounded to a set hour
```

## Silver Bullet — **ESTABLISHED (Tier 1)**

The strongest-attribution item in the whole Phase-3 pass: ICT taught it **by name** inside his own 2022
Mentorship (video titles "ICT AM/PM/London Silver Bullet | 2022 Mentorship Model" exist on his channel).

- **Three one-hour windows (ET):** London **03:00–04:00**, NY AM **10:00–11:00**, NY PM **02:00–03:00**.
- **Mechanic:** liquidity sweep → **Market Structure Shift** (MSS) on 1–3 min → displacement leaves an
  **FVG** → wait for retracement into that FVG → enter in the MSS direction, stop beyond the displacement
  candle, target the next liquidity pool (commonly cited ~1:3 R:R).
- **Edge / why it matters:** a complete, structurally-coherent setup built on Tier-1 primitives (sweep →
  displacement → FVG → MSS). The strict window *is* the discipline — "if it doesn't appear in the hour, you
  don't take it."
- **Failure mode:** chasing the displacement leg instead of waiting for the FVG retracement; trading outside
  the one-hour window.
- **Do NOT repeat as fact:** community "71% win rate on qualified setups" and "55–65%" figures are unsourced
  marketing (SPECULATIVE). The one independent backtest found (15 trades, 45 days) self-flagged as too small
  to claim edge. Treat Silver Bullet as a *disciplined recipe*, not a proven-edge number.

## ICT Macros — **ESTABLISHED concept / community-reconstructed table (Tier 2–3)**

Recurring ~20-minute windows, timed to the clock hour, where ICT teaches the algorithm runs a sweep and/or
rebalances an FVG. The *concept* (hour-anchored delivery windows) is genuine ICT (2023–24 Mentorship); the
**exact master table is a crowd-converged reconstruction** — every online list traces to a community tweet
(@infinitytradeIO), not ICT's own account, and aggregators admit "no video timestamps or episode citations."

Windows appearing consistently across ≥4 sources (ET):

| Window | Time |
|---|---|
| NY AM Macro 1 | 08:50–09:10 |
| **NY AM Macro 2 (flagship)** | **09:50–10:10** |
| NY AM Macro 3 | 10:50–11:10 |
| NY Lunch Macro | 11:50–12:10 |
| NY PM Macro | 13:10–13:40 *(breaks the hour-boundary rule — a named exception)* |
| NY Last-Hour Macro | 15:15–15:45 |
| London Macro 1 / 2 | 02:33–03:00 / 04:03–04:30 |

- **Rule of thumb (best-corroborated):** "macro happens every hour — last 10 min of the closing hour + first
  10 min of the opening hour," with named exceptions.
- **Edge:** timing your *attention*, not a standalone signal — the macro tells you *when* to watch for a
  sweep+FVG, not *that* one will occur. The mechanism (execution clustering at clock/round-number boundaries,
  VWAP/TWAP slicing) is consistent with real microstructure — not pure numerology, but not a trigger either.
- **Failure mode:** "it's 9:50, therefore trade" — treating the window as a signal rather than a filter.
- **Contradiction (flagged):** the 13:10–13:40 PM window breaks the stated hour-boundary rule; some sources
  dispute a 13:50–14:10 window. The table is patched ad hoc — treat as approximate.

## Killzone micro-structure / Judas Swing — **ESTABLISHED sequence / contradictory boundaries (Tier 2)**

Inside a killzone, ICT frames a three-phase sequence: **Accumulation** (pre-session range) →
**Manipulation** (the **Judas Swing** — a deliberate false move against the true bias that sweeps stops) →
**Distribution** (the real move, confirmed by a lower-timeframe MSS that prints the entry FVG).

- **For index futures (NQ/ES/YM),** anchor the Judas Swing to the **09:30 ET equity open**, not the midnight
  forex framing: price runs one way 09:30–~09:50 sweeping premarket/overnight liquidity, then reverses via
  MSS — which is exactly when NY AM Macro 2 (09:50–10:10) fires and inside the 10:00–11:00 Silver Bullet hour.
- **Contradiction (do not smooth):** NY killzone boundaries genuinely disagree by source — **07:00–10:00**
  (forex framing) vs **08:30–11:00** (equities/index framing, anchored to the cash open). For NQ/ES/YM prefer
  the equities-open-anchored version, but treat any single killzone table as approximate; ICT's own killzone
  times have shifted across course versions.
- **Failure mode:** entering *during* the Judas Swing (mistaking manipulation for the real move) instead of
  waiting for the MSS that confirms distribution.

Citations: <https://forum.ictsharks.com/t/ict-forex-market-maker-primer-course-understanding-the-ict-judas-swing/235>
(Tier 2, explicitly not verbatim) · <https://www.smartmoneytrader.co/blog/ict-nq-futures-strategy> (index framing).

## New confluences surfaced

- **Macro/killzone × SMT (double-check):** check SMT non-confirmation *twice* — once **during** the Judas
  Swing (an early tell the manipulation leg is fake, before the MSS) and again **at** distribution
  (confirmation). Most retail material only checks it at the final leg. Ties directly to
  [[concepts/mastery/unified/README]] Layer 2 (the nested SMT stack).
- **The three-zoom stack itself** (killzone → macro → Judas → Silver Bullet) is the pedagogical unification:
  the same setup, told at hour / 20-min / entry granularity. **Label: EMERGING** as a *combined* model.

## How it interacts with the Unified Playbook

Slots into Layer 3 (Execution) as the **time filter**: only take a Layer-2-confirmed setup when it lands
inside a valid killzone → ideally a macro sub-window → executed per Silver Bullet's FVG-retracement rule on a
Silver-Bullet hour. It does **not** replace the entry models — it sharpens *when* they are allowed to fire.
