# Quarterly Theory, 90-Minute Cycles & the True Day Open

A macro **WHEN** model that gives *fixed, non-discretionary* time boundaries for each Power-of-Three phase —
instead of "manipulation happens sometime in the session," you know in advance *which* window is expected to
hold the Judas swing. Extends the session/bias material in
[[concepts/course/module-3-session-and-bias/01-power-of-three]] and [[concepts/business-logic/ict-narratives]].

> **Attribution matters here.** Quarterly Theory is **NOT ICT doctrine.** It was originated by **Jevaunie
> Daye ("Trader Daye", @traderdaye) in March 2023**, extending ICT's Power of Three (AMD) into a 4-quarter
> fractal. Both Phase-3 research agents independently confirmed this; sources credit Daye consistently, and
> tool-builders explicitly disclaim their own authorship ("not an ICT concept... inspired by and used with
> ICT method"). Cite it as Daye's, never as Huddleston's.

## Quarterly Theory — **EMERGING (community-derived, Tier 2)**

A fractal time-cycle: **Year → Month → Week → Day → 6-hr session → 90-min cycle → 22.5-min micro-cycle**,
each level split into 4 quarters following **AMDX** (**A**ccumulation → **M**anipulation → **D**istribution →
**X** = continuation/reversal). The **True Open** = the start of Q2 at every level.

**Daily quarters (ET):**

| Quarter | Session | Window | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Asia | 18:00–00:00 | Accumulation |
| **Q2** | London | **00:00–06:00** | **Manipulation** — *Daily True Open = midnight* |
| Q3 | NY AM | 06:00–12:00 | Distribution |
| Q4 | NY PM | 12:00–18:00 | Continuation/Reversal |

Each 6-hr quarter subdivides into four **90-minute cycles** (→ **16 per day**; 24h×60÷90 = 16, arithmetically
confirmed), each with its own internal True Open (e.g. London's internal True Open ≈ 01:30 ET). **Weekly:**
Mon=Q1, Tue=Q2, Wed=Q3, Thu=Q4; Friday excluded.

- **Edge / why it matters:** fixed boundaries for each PO3 phase — Q2 is specifically where the Judas
  swing/stop-run is expected. The **daily 6-hr / 90-min level is the most solidly corroborated** part; build
  rules there before trusting the week/month/year extensions.
- **Failure mode:** retroactively relabeling whichever quarter "worked" as the manipulation quarter; applying
  the same confidence to the higher fractal levels as to the daily level.
- **Contradictions (do not smooth):** the **Weekly True Open** is disputed — theoretical Monday 18:00 ET vs.
  operationalized as Tuesday's NY open in at least one popular indicator (acknowledged on its own support
  thread). The AMDX quarter-numbering has a majority convention (used above) plus a minority alternate.

## The "True Day Open" — three distinct referents, frequently conflated

A disambiguation footnote worth keeping, because "true day open" is used for at least four different things:

1. **ICT's IPDA True Day — ESTABLISHED (Tier 2).** A 24-hr interbank day opening at **midnight (00:00 ET)**,
   closing at **15:00 ET** ("IPDA True Day Close" — this is what "New York Close" means in ICT's material, not
   the 16:00 equity close). The **Midnight Open** is ICT's primary daily-bias pivot (price above/below =
   bullish/bearish lean); an *independent* backtest (not ICT's) found 60–67% retracement-to-midnight-open on YM.
2. **The community "18:00 ET = true day open" claim — SPECULATIVE-or-FRINGE.** A documentable **conflation**
   of two real-but-different things: ICT's **New Day Opening Gap (NDOG)** — a literal ES/NQ/YM price gap from
   the CME 17:00–18:00 ET maintenance halt — and CME Globex's convention that an 18:00 session start counts
   toward the *next* trade date. **Neither is ICT teaching 18:00 as the daily-bias open.** One Tier-3 source
   uses "Midnight Open" (00:00) and "True Day Open" (18:00) *in the same article* — term-conflation, not doctrine.
3. **Quarterly-Theory True Opens (Daye).** Daily True Open = midnight; Weekly True Open = Monday 18:00 — these
   answer **different questions** (daily vs. weekly anchor), not rival candidates for the same point.

**Ruling:** use the **Midnight Open (00:00 ET)** as the daily bias filter; use the **NDOG (17:00–18:00 ET
gap)** purely as a discrete fillable target. Do not let the "6pm = true day open" mislabel into an execution rule.

## New confluence surfaced

> **Q2 × killzone agreement.** Quarterly Theory's 90-min Q2 sub-windows land *near* ICT's killzones — London
> Q2 (01:30–03:00 ET) sits just before/inside the London killzone (02:00–05:00); NY-AM Q2 (07:30–09:00) sits
> at the start of the NY killzone. The frameworks are **independently authored**, so rough agreement is real
> evidence, not circularity — **but they do not nest exactly.** Treat a Q2 window landing inside a killzone as
> a stronger manipulation signal than either alone, *require* the IPDA/CBDR level to be what is actually swept,
> and *require* SMT non-confirmation before calling the reversal real. **Label: EMERGING.**
