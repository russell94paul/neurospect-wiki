# Aura Asset

The **Aura Asset** is dOoMeR's name for the Swiss Franc futures contract (CME ticker **6S**),
which he adds as a universal fourth leg to every triad in his system alongside the standard three
correlated assets. The name is also the source of the mentorship/playlist title. dOoMeR is explicit
that the *reasoning* behind the pick is a personal, admittedly speculative essay — not the
quantitative correlation work — but the *selection itself* was validated against 20 years of charts
before being adopted (aura-15). This page captures both halves: the reasoning as dOoMeR tells it, and
the chart-based justification he lands on.

## Definition

The Aura Asset **is** the Swiss Franc futures contract, 6S. dOoMeR uses "Aura Asset" when talking
about what it represents in his framework, and "Swiss Franc" or "6S" when talking about the
instrument itself. It is added to **every triad** in the system (metals, indices, energy, forex,
crypto) as an extra divergence leg, functioning as a dollar-reference asset that — unlike DXY — is a
properly time-synchronized futures contract (aura-15).

## Origin and Reasoning (dOoMeR's Own Framing)

dOoMeR is unusually explicit that this section is a personal thesis, not a claim of fact:

> "I am not presenting any of what follows as fact. I'm presenting it as the chain of thinking that
> led me to what I call the Aura Asset. The name comes first, the asset itself comes later. Once the
> reasoning is laid out, at that point I went and checked the charts and the charts did the rest of
> the talking." (aura-15)

That ordering — name and reasoning first, chart validation second — is itself worth noting: it is the
opposite of how he describes the *triad correlation document* being built (Pearson correlation first,
then adoption — see [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]]).

### Gold, Elite Families, and the Springmeier Document

The chain starts with gold. dOoMeR's premise: gold is not just a commodity but a currency held
specifically by "elite families... very high-net-worth individuals, and dynasties going back to the
beginning of recorded time" — the group he says has survived every currency collapse, war, and regime
change by holding gold (aura-15).

While discussing this with a contact referred to in the transcript only by a name captioned as
**"Niger"** (uncertain transcription — likely a mis-heard proper name or handle; not confidently
correctable and left as-is), dOoMeR was sent a PDF: *Bloodlines of the Illuminati*, attributed to
"Fritz Springer" in the transcript — corrected here to **Fritz Springmeier**, the actual author of
that book, since the auto-caption mis-hearing is unambiguous (aura-15).

A passage in that document about Switzerland is what dOoMeR credits as the trigger for the entire
chain of reasoning: that Switzerland's centuries of neutrality through European wars is not (only) a
function of diplomacy, geography, and armed neutrality, but of long-standing, deliberate protection by
the same elite bloodlines that hold gold — because Switzerland is where their wealth is kept.

### From Country to Currency

dOoMeR's stated logic: **if** Switzerland is a country whose stability is protected by the wealth that
sits inside it, **then** the currency of that country should behave like a protected asset — it should
mirror the strongest safe havens, and move in ways that don't make sense unless something is holding
it steady (aura-15). Unlike the Switzerland-elite-protection premise itself, this consequence is
checkable on a chart — and that check is what the rest of the paper is about.

He is careful to hedge the underlying claim on both sides:

> "This can be explained two ways. One, it is a coincidence. 200 years of neutrality, mountainous
> terrain that is hard to invade, a well-armed population, careful diplomacy... that is the boring
> explanation and is probably most of the story. Two, it goes deeper than that..." (aura-15)

## Why the Swiss Franc, Not DXY

Everything traded in the system is priced against the US dollar — gold vs. dollar, Bitcoin vs. dollar,
every major FX pair vs. dollar, even equity indices as dollar-denominated prices of the world's
biggest companies (aura-15). That makes the dollar the de facto reference for every chart in the
system, which raises the question dOoMeR says "nobody seems to ask": why isn't there a clean way to
analyze the dollar itself directly, on the same footing as everything else traded?

The standard answer, **DXY (US Dollar Index)**, is where he identifies a structural problem:

- DXY is not a futures contract — it's a weighted basket of currencies, calculated and reported by an
  exchange, whose candles do **not** open and close in sync with the futures contracts actually
  traded in the system (gold, indices, currency futures).
- dOoMeR frames this as a direct contradiction of the system's own premises: "This entire methodology
  is built around time. Time-based divergence, time-based sweeps, time-based confirmations... but if
  you are willing to analyze an asset whose candles do not open and close in sync with the ones you
  are actually trading, then you do not really value time as much as you say you do." (aura-15)

**The Swiss Franc (6S) solves the synchronization problem.** It is a properly listed CME futures
contract that opens and closes on the same bar as gold, the indices, and the other currency futures in
the system — while still tracking DXY's shape closely, especially on daily/weekly timeframes, where
"the noise phases out and the structural dollar direction shows through" (aura-15). It is not an exact
replica of DXY (DXY is a multi-currency basket; 6S is a single currency), but it moves inversely to the
dollar with unusual tightness — "closer than almost any other major pair" — and does so on a clock
synchronized to the rest of the system's assets.

## Observed Chart Behavior (the Validation)

Independent of which version of the Switzerland thesis is true, dOoMeR states the market behavior is
what actually earns 6S its place in the system. He lists the Aura Asset as observed to:

- Mirror the dollar (inverse correlation, tight, especially on higher timeframes)
- Rally during stress/risk-off conditions
- Track gold
- Move against risk assets, consistently

> "I am not claiming I know who or what protects Switzerland. I am saying the chart looks the way it
> would look if something did. And that is enough for this paper." (aura-15)

Those properties — behaving like a protected, safe-haven, dollar-inverse asset, on a synchronized
clock — are what dOoMeR says make it "a good addition to a correlation-based system," and are the
stated basis for putting it on every triad.

## Scope of Use

The Aura Asset is applied across **every triad** in the system (metals, indices, forex, energy,
crypto — see [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]]), but its influence is not uniform:

- Strongest / most respected: indices (NASDAQ/NQ-ES-YM) triad, metals ("bullion") triad, forex triad
- Weakest: energy (oil) triad — dOoMeR attributes this to oil being "a very manipulated" asset, making
  it "a tougher asset to trade without the Aura Asset just alone itself" (aura-16)

How the Aura Asset is actually read on a chart — as a divergence leg feeding the same Sequential SMT
framework used on the standard triads — is covered in [[concepts/aura/time-sum]]. The applied,
top-down workflow for using it from higher timeframe down to lower timeframe is covered in
[[concepts/aura/htf-ltf-application]].

## Open Questions / dOoMeR's Own Caveats

have put together on my own... I am not presenting any of what follows as fact") and should be read as
his reasoning process, separate from the quantitative triad-selection work.
