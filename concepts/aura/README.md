> flags an uncertainty or reconstruction, the transcript wins.

## What makes Aura distinct

Aura is an ICT/SMC framework, but it diverges from the MrWitness-AXL corpus in three ways:

1. **Psychology-first.** The mentorship spends its first five sessions on mindset, emotional
   control, discipline systems, and journaling *before* any chart concept — heavily drawing on
   Tom Dante, Mark Douglas, *Atomic Habits* (James Clear), and Huberman. See the Psychology block.
2. **A proprietary SMT layer.** Ordinary SMT is filtered through **time-cycle nesting**
   ("Sequential SMT"), with a formal, math-first (Pearson-correlation) method for selecting the
   correlated-asset "Triads" it runs on.
3. **The Aura Asset.** A signature fourth leg — **Swiss Franc futures (6S)** — added to every
   triad as a dollar-proxy divergence tell.
