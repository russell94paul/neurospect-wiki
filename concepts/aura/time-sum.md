# Time Sum

This page covers dOoMeR's last new technical topic in the curriculum, drawn from a single video that
covers two unrelated things: the **Time Sum** ("369") numerology concept, and the mechanics of **using
the [[concepts/aura/aura-asset]]** as a divergence tool (aura-16). The two halves matter very
differently: dOoMeR explicitly de-emphasizes Time Sum as a minor, subjective, rarely-used confluence,
while treating the Aura Asset as central and highly influential. That contrast is preserved here
deliberately rather than treating both as equally load-bearing.

## Time Sum Defined

**Time Sum** is the operation of adding together the digits of a candle's timestamp (as a 4-digit,
24-hour value) and repeating the addition until a single digit remains — i.e. its digital root. dOoMeR
calls the broader pattern **"369"**: certain times reduce to 3, 6, or 9, and he treats those as the
"coincidences of time within the markets" worth noting (aura-16).

### Mechanics (Worked Examples from the Transcript)

| Time | Digits | Sum | Reduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18:00 (futures market open) | 1, 8, 0, 0 | 9 | **9** |
| 09:30 (NY cash/equity open) | 0, 9, 3, 0 | 12 | 1 + 2 = **3** |

dOoMeR notes both the futures market open and the NY 9:30 open reduce to 369-family digits, and cites
this as one instance of the pattern (aura-16). A TradingView indicator (author's handle captured in
the transcript as **"notary"** — uncertain transcription, not confidently correctable) automates
flagging candles whose Time Sum is 3, 6, or 9.

### Proposed Use — and Its Limits

The stated use case: check whether **swing points** form on candles whose Time Sum is 3, 6, or 9.
dOoMeR is explicit about the limits of this as a signal:

- Not every 3/6/9 candle forms a swing point, and not every swing point lands on a 3/6/9 candle.
- Widening the net (checking 3, *then also* 6, *then also* 9, *then also* candles immediately after a
  369 candle) increases how many candles could plausibly match — which he identifies himself as a
  path toward unfalsifiable, hindsight-driven pattern-matching: "there's so many hindsight models you
  can make up that I don't find extreme value within it." (aura-16)

## dOoMeR's Own Weighting (De-emphasis)

dOoMeR states he used to research 369 in depth but shifted his attention toward aligning the daily and
session cycle with 1H/4H SMT, the two-stage model, the weekly-range model, and — combined with the
Aura Asset — found more value there than in continuing to chase 369 (aura-16). His framing is direct
and repeated:

> "This does not hold more power than anything else that I've ever taught you. This would be an
> extra, maybe rarely added confluence, but again it's very subjective how you want to use it." (aura-16)

He also pre-empts the idea that he is sitting on a hidden 369-based edge, denying it explicitly and at
length:

> "I am not hiding something from you that is related to 369 that I believe has cracked the code and I
> have made millions of dollars off of this... I did not make millions off of it. This is not a secret
> hidden theory that I have discovered." (aura-16)

As a personal aside (not a trading rule), he notes his own futures contract sizes and the mentorship's
price were deliberately chosen to sum to 3, 6, or 9 — a personal habit, offered as color rather than
technique.

**Contrast with the Aura Asset:** in the same breath as downplaying 369, dOoMeR reaffirms the opposite
for the Aura Asset — "the Aura Asset is very influential" — making the emphasis gap between the two
halves of this video explicit in his own words (aura-16).

## Using the Aura Asset

The second half of the video (and the primary point of the "using the Aura Asset" half of its title)
covers mechanics: **the Aura Asset (6S) is read exactly like a normal divergence leg within a triad.**
It gets fed into the same divergence framework already taught for the standard triads — watched for
**weekly cycle**, **daily cycle**, and **session cycle** divergence (i.e., a Sequential-SMT-style
divergence detected at that timeframe — see [[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]]) — just with the Aura
Asset added as an extra comparison leg alongside the triad's usual three assets.

### Worked Example 1 — Explaining Chop

dOoMeR walks a period of consolidating, "up and down, up and down, up and down" price action where the
**standard triad showed no weekly-cycle divergence at all** — nothing in the normal triad explained
the chop. Overlaying the Aura Asset revealed a repeating pattern instead: a weekly cycle divergence
above, then one below, then one back above — each flip corresponding to a leg of the chop (aura-16).

> "Without this, you wouldn't have seen any of this — the weekly or the daily. You would have been
> confused why we had this explosive move." (aura-16)

### Worked Example 2 — Explaining a Reversal Sequence

A second example: a daily-cycle divergence drove a move lower; a weekly-cycle divergence then formed;
price reversed higher through two quarters back into discount; a further daily-cycle divergence then
drove another move down. None of these divergence lines were visible on the standard triad — only on
the Aura Asset overlay (referred to in the transcript as "the aura label") (aura-16).

### An Edge Case (Bridge to Sequential Skip)

dOoMeR notes a rarer case where a Sequential-SMT-style line can continue to "hold" even when **not
every asset in the triad** takes the same higher low at a quarter's close — he flags this as feeding
into what he separately calls Sequential Skip rather than a standard read (see
[[concepts/aura/sequential-smt]]).

### Bridge to Filtering (Next Lesson)

dOoMeR flags that dropping to lower timeframes multiplies how many Aura Asset / Sequential SMT
divergences appear, and that filtering them uses tools already taught — higher-timeframe market
structure and premium/discount — rather than anything new. The full applied workflow is covered in
[[concepts/aura/htf-ltf-application]].

## Scope and Reliability by Asset

The Aura Asset "works for all assets," but reliability varies by triad (aura-16):

- Most respected: NASDAQ (indices) triad, forex triad, and bullion (metals) triad — the transcript's
  "bulge triad" is corrected here to **bullion triad**, an auto-caption mis-hearing consistent with the
  metals/gold-silver-platinum triad referenced elsewhere in the corpus (see
  [[concepts/aura/triads-asset-selection]]).
- Least respected: the energy (oil) triad — described as "a very manipulated" and "tougher asset to
  trade without the Aura Asset... alone itself."
